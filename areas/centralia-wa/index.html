Professional Excavation & Site Development in Centralia, WA

Centralia occupies a prominent position in Lewis County — an Amtrak Cascades stop, a historic downtown, and a growing mix of residential, commercial, and light-industrial development along the I-5 corridor. Excavation work here ranges from infill construction on tight downtown parcels to larger-scale site clearing on the city’s expanding edges. Soil conditions vary from compacted urban fill near the historic core to softer alluvial soils closer to the Chehalis River watershed. Brynion Excavation evaluates each Centralia site carefully, designing a grading and drainage approach that fits the specific terrain before excavation begins.

Why Centralia Builders and Landowners Rely on Brynion Excavation

Centralia projects typically require Lewis County or City of Centralia grading and building permits, and earthwork near the Chehalis River floodplain may involve Washington Department of Ecology stormwater oversight. Brynion Excavation is a licensed Washington contractor that understands these overlapping local and state requirements. We prepare erosion and sediment control plans, assist with permit documentation where needed, and follow all applicable regulations to protect your project and the surrounding environment.
